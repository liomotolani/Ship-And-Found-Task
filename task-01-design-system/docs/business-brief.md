# Business Brief

## 1. Business name

VetReply.

## 2. Who uses it

**Receptionist (main user)**

- Age: 18 and over.
- Device: computer at the practice (Job 4: "confident using computers, email and office systems").
- Internet quality: Assumption (no source): reliable practice broadband.
- How technical: confident with computers, email, office systems and the practice management system (Job 4). Not described as technical beyond that.

**Practice Manager**

- Age: 18 and over.
- Device: Assumption (no source): computer.
- Internet quality: Assumption (no source): reliable practice broadband.
- How technical: sound IT knowledge and experience using different programmes (Job 1).

**Client (pet owner)**

- Age: 18 and over.
- Device: mobile phone and computer.
- Internet quality: Assumption (no source): varies, so pages must work on a slower connection.
- How technical: Assumption (no source): non-technical.

## 3. The one thing that must work

When a receptionist asks a question, the assistant returns an answer drawn only from documents their role is allowed to see, shows the source, and says so when nothing relevant is found (raw idea). It must be quick enough to use during a live call or while a client waits at the desk (raw idea).

## 4. Three questions customers ask repeatedly

The job descriptions list reception duties, not actual customer questions. These three are inferred from those duties and need validating with a practice.

1. Can I book or change an appointment? (Job 2, Job 4: booking, amending and managing appointments)
2. How do I pay? (Job 2, Job 4: taking payments and processing transactions)
3. Are you open on Saturdays? (Job 4: receptionist works 1 in 4 Saturday mornings, 9am to 12pm. That the practice is open to clients then is inferred.)

## 5. How the app should feel

- Calm and professional, matching how receptionists are expected to work under pressure (Job 4).
- Empathetic in drafted replies, since staff handle sensitive or emotional situations (Job 4).
- Fast and uncluttered, because it is used in a busy environment while managing several tasks at once (Job 2, Job 4).
- Trustworthy: every answer shows its source, and nothing reaches a client without staff approval (raw idea).
